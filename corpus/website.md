# School website

The website is the first place parents look for news, the term calendar and fees information. Content must be accurate, approved and up to date.

## Publishing a news post

Write the post in a shared document first and send it for approval. Each post needs a clear title, the date of the event, two or three short paragraphs and at most four photos.

Once approved, sign in to the website dashboard, create a new post in the News category, paste the text, add the photos and publish. Share the link on the school's social media pages afterwards.

## Updating the term calendar

At the start of each term, replace the calendar page with the dates confirmed by the administration: opening and closing days, exams, sports days and parents' meetings.

Remove past events and mark any date that is still to be confirmed. Parents plan travel and payments around these dates, so a wrong date causes real problems.

## Photos and learner privacy

Only publish photos of learners whose parents have given consent on the media consent form. Never publish a learner's full name together with their photo.

Resize photos to about 1200 pixels wide before uploading. Large photos make pages slow, especially for parents browsing on mobile data.

## Who approves content

The head or the deputy head approves every post before it goes live. Fees information and official notices are approved by the bursar as well.

Keep a simple log of what was published, when and who approved it, so any question about website content can be traced.
