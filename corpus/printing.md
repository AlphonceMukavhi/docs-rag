# Printing

Most printing problems at school come from three things: the printer is not on the network, the wrong printer is selected, or the paper path is blocked. Check these before calling for help.

## Printer shows offline

If a laptop says the printer is offline, first look at the printer itself. It must be switched on, show no error light and be connected to the staff Wi-Fi or the network cable.

On the laptop, open Settings, then Printers and scanners, and make sure the school office printer is set as the default. Remove any old copies of the same printer, because Windows often keeps a duplicate that points to an old IP address.

If it is still offline, switch the printer off for thirty seconds and on again, then restart the print spooler service on the laptop.

## Clearing a paper jam

Switch the printer off before opening any covers. Open the front tray and the back access door and pull the paper out slowly in the direction it normally travels, so it does not tear.

Check for small torn pieces. A tiny scrap left inside will cause the next jam. Use paper of the right weight and fan the stack before loading it, because damp or stuck-together sheets jam more often.

## Adding a printer to a new laptop

Connect the laptop to the staff Wi-Fi first. Then open Settings, Printers and scanners, and choose Add device. Pick the office printer from the list and print a test page.

If the printer does not appear, add it by its IP address, which is printed on the network configuration page. Hold the printer's information button for five seconds to print that page.

## Toner and ink levels

Order a new toner cartridge when the printer reports it is low, not when it is empty. Delivery to the school can take a week.

Record each cartridge change in the equipment log with the date, so we can see how many pages each cartridge really lasts and plan the printing budget for the term.
