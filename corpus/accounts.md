# Accounts and passwords

Every staff member has one school Google Workspace account for email, Drive, Forms and Classroom. Accounts are personal and must never be shared.

## Resetting a staff password

A staff member who forgets their password should ask the IT office in person or by phone, never by email from another account. Confirm who they are before doing anything.

In the Google Admin console, open the user, choose Reset password, create a temporary password and tick the option that forces a change at next sign-in. Give the temporary password to the person directly.

## Turning on two-step verification

Two-step verification protects an account even when a password is stolen. Every staff account that handles learner records or school money must use it.

The staff member signs in, opens Manage your Google Account, then Security, and turns on 2-Step Verification. The Google Authenticator app or a phone prompt is better than SMS codes.

## Setting up a new staff member

Create the account using the format firstname.surname at the school domain. Add the person to the correct staff group so they receive the right shared folders and calendars.

Give them the welcome guide, which explains the acceptable use policy, how to join staff Wi-Fi and how to turn on two-step verification in their first week.

## When a staff member leaves

On the last working day, suspend the account rather than deleting it. Transfer ownership of their Drive files to the head of department.

Change any shared passwords they knew, such as the guest Wi-Fi password, and remove them from groups. Delete the account only after ninety days, once nothing else depends on it.
